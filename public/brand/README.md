# Company brand assets

Original logo and icons downloaded from Premier Centre Group's official website, https://pcgroup.com.my/, on 29 October 2026 for the company ePTW application.

- `pcg-logo.png`: https://pcgroup.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/PCG-logo.png
- `pcg-icon-192.png`: https://pcgroup.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/cropped-PCG-favicon-192x192.png
- `pcg-apple-icon.png`: https://pcgroup.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/cropped-PCG-favicon-180x180.png

Artwork is preserved without recolouring or changing its proportions. The company's website styles specify primary green `#004e46`, teal `#6ea4a2`, mint `#acd5cf` and warm neutral `#c5aba1`. The application uses these colours for its visual theme and keeps safety status colours separate.

Rubik, the typeface used by the company website, is self-hosted in `src/app/fonts`. Its SIL Open Font License is included there.
